Questions

Before we start.

How long does a website take?

Most websites take around 1–4 weeks depending on the number of pages, features and how quickly content and feedback are available.

Can I add features later?

Yes. GrowOrbits is designed around growth, so you can start with the essentials and add features as your business needs change.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. We can guide you through domain and hosting setup and help get the project ready for launch.

Can I choose only the services I need?

Absolutely. Orbit Custom is specifically designed around your requirements and budget.

What happens after launch?

You can continue with a support plan for updates, technical assistance, content changes and future improvements.
